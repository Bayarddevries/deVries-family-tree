Stories & Profiles

James Spence Sr — Orkney fur trader · James Spence Sr

Born c1753 in Birsay, Orkney. Came to York Factory in 1773 as an HBC labourer and inland voyageur ('a fine fellow and a good canoe man'). Left in charge of Buckingham House in 1793, the HBC's most westerly post at the time. Died there aged 42 in 1795, leaving Margaret pregnant with their fourth child.

Margaret 'Nestichio' Batt — the Métis matriarch · Margaret 'Nestichio' Batt

Daughter of Isaac Batt, an English inland trader, and a Cree woman. Family story: as a young girl she was kidnapped by the Blackfeet; James Spence rescued her using a relay of horses, then waited to court her until she came of age. She became the Métis root of this family, dying at Red River in 1829.

source ↗
Andrew Setter — Orkney voyageur · Andrew Setter

Born 1777 in Westray, Orkney; joined the HBC at York Factory in 1800 as a labourer. For 18 years he voyaged inland. In 1821 Rev. John West baptised and married him to Margaret Spence at Beaver Creek — the first HBC clergyman's marriage west of Red River. Retired to St Andrews Parish in 1823.

source ↗
David Spence — politician & MLA · David Spence

Born 1824 at Red River. A Métis leader: delegate for St. Anne's/Poplar Point to the 1870 Convention of Forty, first MLA for Poplar Point 1870-74, and a Justice of the Peace. With his wife Catherine he claimed Half-breed Scrip in 1875. Father of 7, home base Poplar Point / Portage la Prairie.

source ↗
Elizabeth Setter — married a Premier · Elizabeth Setter

Daughter of George Setter; in 1862 married John Norquay, who in 1878 became Manitoba's first Métis Premier. Through her, this family connects to Manitoba's provincial founding.

source ↗
Premier John Norquay — cousin by marriage · Premier John Norquay

Manitoba's first Métis Premier (1878-87). Married Elizabeth Setter, daughter of George Setter. A towering figure in early Manitoba politics.

source ↗
Gerhard & Geeske De Vries — Dutch settlers · Gerhard De Vries

Dutch immigrants from Friesland, Netherlands. Married 4 Oct 1929 in Ochre River MB; homesteaded on the south shore of Lake Dauphin in the Turtle River District. Raised 11 children on the farm.

source ↗
Bryon Edward deVries — HR Director, family man · Bryon Edward deVries

Born 17 Jul 1954; died 13 Oct 2019. Director of Human Resources at Sunrise School Division (and previously WRHA). Remembered for hosting Sunday family dinners for 20+ years, coaching soccer, and his devotion to helping anyone.

source ↗
Mavis Irene (Hamilton) Lau · Mavis Irene Hamilton

Born 1 Sep 1933 in Tisdale SK; moved to Flin Flon at 6. Only daughter of six children. A remarkable seamstress, knitter, cross-stitcher, curler, golfer and bridge player; worked at Sears in Flin Flon until retirement.
