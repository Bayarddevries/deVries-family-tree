ELAINE MARGARET FLEURY
July 29, 1960 - November 19, 2025

Source: Alterna Cremation obituary
URL: https://www.alternacremation.ca/obituaries/Elaine-Margaret-Fleury?obId=46506704
Also published in Winnipeg Free Press Passages (id-333938), passed at Grace Hospital, Winnipeg.

---- OBITUARY TEXT ----

It is with great sadness that the family announces the sudden passing of Elaine Fleury on
November 19, 2025, at Grace Hospital in Winnipeg, Manitoba.

Born on July 29, 1960, to Charles and Florence Fleury, Elaine was a mother, daughter, sister,
and friend, and will be remembered as a free spirit with a beautiful soul - someone who loved
deeply, laughed loudly, and brought light to those around her. Her warm nature, sense of
adventure, and playful spirit shone through in the small joys she loved - playing air guitar,
going to bingo, and perfecting her eyeliner. She will be fondly remembered for her natural
beauty, photogenic smile, kindness, and humour.

The family extends heartfelt thanks to the staff of Oakview Place and to the doctors, nurses,
and staff of the Intensive Care Unit at Grace Hospital for their compassionate care.

A private service will be held for immediate family, followed by a luncheon for extended family
and friends in the lower level of St. Paul the Apostle Church, 2400 Portage Avenue, Winnipeg
at 12:00PM on December 4, 2025.
