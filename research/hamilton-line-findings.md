## 2. Hamilton family origins & migration → Tisdale SK / Flin Flon

**Migration path (reconstructed from births/deaths/residences):**
1. **Ontario:** John James Hamilton born 1856, Mornington Twp, **Perth County, Ontario** (settler family). ✅
2. **→ Manitoba:** by c.1878–1880 he had moved west to the **Beautiful Plains** region of western Manitoba (Guy born there 1882; "Beautiful Plains" is the Neepawa/Gladstone area). ✅
3. **→ Saskatchewan (Tisdale):** the family moved to the **Tisdale, SK** homestead district — John James died there **1913**, Jane died there **1931**; Guy lived in Prince Albert (1911) and Melfort, dying in Tisdale **1946**; son **Lawrence Donald was born in Tisdale 15 Jun 1912**. ✅
4. **→ Flin Flon, MB:** Lawrence Donald Hamilton married Doris Setter (in Tisdale) and the next generation settled in **Flin Flon, Manitoba/Saskatchewan border** (Lawrence died Flin Flon 5 Feb 1984; daughter Mavis b. Tisdale 1933). ✅

**Key detail added:** The previously-open **Setter→Hamilton marriage** is now dated — **Lawrence Donald Hamilton ⚭ Doris Alberta Setter, 29 Dec 1932, Tisdale, SK** (FamilySearch Lawrence Donald MW7Z-8LG ✅; not in a public SK/MB index, hence secondary-corroborated). This closes the gap flagged in `vital-stats-findings.md`.

**Non-Métis note:** The Hamiltons are an Ontario→Manitoba→Saskatchewan **British settler** family. They enter the tree only through Lawrence Donald's marriage to Métis **Doris Alberta Setter** — so **all Bayard's Métis ancestry flows through Doris (Riggs/Spence), not through the Hamiltons.** ✅ (Consistent with the tree's documented maternal-only Métis rule.)
