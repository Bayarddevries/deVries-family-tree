# Spence Sibling Families — Research Findings

Scope: Build out the 7 children of **David Spence (1824–1885)** & **Catherine Hallett (1824–1880)**, Poplar Point, Red River (Métis). Focus = non-Riggs sibling marriages (Brown, Folster, Wishart) + John J., Charles D., William Spence. Riggs branch already in tree.

Status key: **VERIFIED** = explicit in cited source. **INFERENCE** = inferred/partial, needs confirmation. Sources all FREE (Red River Ancestry / redriverancestry.ca, MHS Memorable Manitobans, Convention of Forty PDF, FamilySearch free tree, MyHeritage/Ancestry snippets).

## 0. Baseline — David & Catherine, the 7 children (VERIFIED)
Sources: redriverancestry.ca/SPENCE-DAVID-1834.php; mhs.mb.ca/docs/people/spence_d.shtml; metismuseum.ca Convention-of-Forty PDF.

Married 15 Feb 1844, St John's, Red River. Catherine = dau. of Catherine PARENTEAU & Henry HALLETT (1799–1871).

1. **John "Jake" Spence** — b. 14 Aug 1848 — m. Ellen McNAB (1870, St James). VERIFIED
2. **Ellen Anderson Spence** — b. 1 Jan 1853 — m. George BROWN (7 Mar 1873, Poplar Point). VERIFIED
3. **Charles David Spence** — b. 9 Feb 1857 — m. Elizabeth "Eliza" FOULDS (Jun 1885, Poplar Point). VERIFIED
4. **Jane Spence** — b. 26 May 1859 — m. William FOLSTER (1879, Poplar Point). VERIFIED
5. **Mary Ann (Marion) Spence** — b. 8 Aug 1861 — m. Ernest Charles RIGGS (1887, Portage la Prairie) — IN TREE ALREADY
6. **Harriet Spence** — b. 17 Jun 1863 — m. Peter Henry WISHART (3 Mar 1887, Poplar Point). VERIFIED
7. **William "Bill" Spence** — b. 13 Jul 1866 — m. Martha McNAB (1909, Portage la Prairie). VERIFIED

> NOTE: The "David, age 13" in the 1870 Poplar Point census is **Charles David Spence** (b.1857), not a separate 8th child. 1881 census lists widower David with Charles(24), Mary Ann(19), Harriet(16), William(14) — exactly 7 children. David d. 16 Sep 1885 (accidentally shot, amputation of leg); Catherine d. 16 Jan 1880.
