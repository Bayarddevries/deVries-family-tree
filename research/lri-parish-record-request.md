**Subject:** Parish-record lookup request (deVries / Spence family genealogy)

Hello,

I'm working on my family's genealogy and hoping the Louis Riel Institute genealogy team can help with a few parish-record lookups. My family is a Red River Métis line from the Poplar Point, High Bluff, and St. François Xavier area, with Spence, Setter, Hallett, and Parenteau surnames. A few gaps need primary records.

Could you help find:

1. **Catherine Parenteau's parentage.** She married Henry Hallett Jr around 1824 at St. John's. We do not yet have a primary record naming her parents. A baptism or marriage entry in the St. John's or St. Boniface parish registers would close this gap.

2. **The given name of the Cree matriarch** who was the mother of Margaret "Nestichio" Batt (daughter of Isaac Batt). A baptism record for Margaret Batt at St. John's or York Factory might name her mother.

3. **Confirmation of the marriage between James Spence Sr and Margaret Batt** (late 1700s, York Factory / Red River), ideally from HBCA or parish records.

4. **The circumstances of David Spence's death** in September 1885 at Poplar Point. The family has two versions (an accidental shooting and a justice-of-the-peace dispute). A death registration, coroner's record, or a 1885 newspaper account would help.

I appreciate your time. Happy to provide any reference numbers or family details that would help. Thank you.
