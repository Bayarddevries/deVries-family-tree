## 2. deVries family origins & where they settled

### Settlement — ✅ VERIFIED (multiple obits)
- The family settled in **Ochre River Rural Municipality, Manitoba** (Turtle River District, just north of Lake Dauphin / Dauphin). All 11 children were raised "of Ochre River, MB."
- ✅ **Leonard de Vries obit (Sneath-Strilchuk, Dauphin):** "born February 27, 1932 on the south shore of **Lake Dauphin**... Growing up in the **Turtle River District**... Grace Baptist Church in **Ochre River, MB**... private interment at the **Turtle River Cemetery**." — https://www.sneathstrilchuk.com/obituary/Leonard-DeVries
- ✅ **John de Vries obit:** family "of Ochre River, MB." — https://passages.winnipegfreepress.com/passage-details/id-118933/VRIES_JOHN

### Origins — ✅ name is Dutch/Frisian; ⚑ this branch emigrated from EAST FRISIA, GERMANY
- **"de Vries" / "de Vriese"** is a Dutch/Frisian **ethnic surname meaning "the Frisian"** — one of the most common surnames in the Netherlands, indicating origin from Friesland. — ✅ https://en.geneanet.org/surnames/de%20VRIES ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Vries
- **BUT this particular Ochre River branch came from East Frisia (Ostfriesland), Germany — not the Netherlands proper.** East Frisia is the historically Frisian/Low-German corner of NW Germany bordering the Netherlands, where "de Vries" and patronymic naming were common.
  - **Geeske (Jessie) Oltrop** was born in **Ditzumerverlaat, East Frisia** (⚑ Geneanet), and her parents Jan Oltrop & Antje Von Lengen in **Bunderhammerich / Weener-Ems, East Frisia** (⚑).
  - Geneanet lists **numerous East Frisian de Vries families from the exact same villages** (Ditzumerverlaat, Bunderhammrich, Weener, Simonswolde) — corroborating that the de Vries clan alongside the Oltrops came from that same corner of Ostfriesland. — ⚑ https://en.geneanet.org/fonds/individus/?go=1&nom=de+VRIES&place%5B0%5D=Ostfriesland
- **Emigration window (INFERENCE):** Gerhard (b. ~1901) and the Oltrops (b. 1885–1914) emigrated from East Frisia to Manitoba in the **~1920s**, before Gerhard & Geeske's 1929 marriage in Ochre River. Likely post-WWI emigration, matching the wave of East Frisian settlement around Dauphin/Ochre River. (Passenger records on paywalled/Ancestry sites — not consulted.)

### Note vs. the rest of the tree
- The paternal deVries line is **European/Dutch-Frisian-German** and entirely distinct from Bayard's **Métis maternal line** (Spence → Setter → Hamilton → Lau). There is **no Métis/Indigenous ancestry** in the deVries surname branch — it is a 20th-century immigrant family. The roster's "MÉTIS" tag on Bayard comes from the maternal side.
