# Scrip (LAC) & HBCA Service Record Findings — Spence / Setter / Riggs / Hallett family

**Research date:** 12 Aug 2026
**Purpose:** Compile Metis scrip (LAC RG15) and HBCA service records for the deVries/Spence maternal line; flag any OCCUPATION = hunter/buffalo-hunt mentions.
**Method:** LAC Collection Search (recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca), HBCA Biographical Sheets (Archives of Manitoba), Dictionary of Canadian Biography, Metis Museum (Barkwell), WikiTree Metis Scrip category, redriverancestry.

**Legend:** [VERIFIED] = primary/archival record (LAC, HBCA, DCB) captured directly. [PLAUSIBLE/INFERENCE] = secondary or hobbyist source; verify before relying on it.

---

## KEY FINDING ON OCCUPATION = "HUNTER"

> **No primary record found in this search names any of these ancestors as a "hunter," "buffalo hunter," or as involved in the pemmican/buffalo hunt.**
>
> - The **LAC scrip record descriptions (RG15-D-II-8-a / -c) do NOT carry an occupation field in their metadata** — the occupation line (when present) is typed on the *physical affidavit image* inside each volume/reel. To confirm the occupation actually written on each affidavit (e.g. "hunter," "farmer," "trader"), one must view the **digitized affidavit image** (LAC lists 3-4 digital objects per file) or the microfilm reel. This was **not** read for this pass.
> - The **HBCA biographical sheets** for James Spence Sr, Andrew Setter, and Isaac Batt give explicit positions, and **none is "hunter"** (see Part 2).
> - **David Spence (1824-1885)** — the only one of this family for whom a biography was consulted in detail — was a political figure (Métis MLA / Convention delegate / Justice of the Peace); no hunter occupation appears in the Barkwell biography.
>
> **Honest bottom line:** the buffalo-hunt/hunter occupation for this family is NOT confirmed by any primary record captured here. If the "hunter" claim appears in family lore or hobbyist trees, it is currently **unverified** and would need to be confirmed by reading the digitized scrip affidavit occupation field and/or the parish/census records. Do not assert it as fact.

### 1. James Spence Sr (b. ca 1754, Birsay, Orkney; d. 27 Nov 1795, Buckingham House, Alberta) — [VERIFIED]
- **Source:** HBCA Biographical Sheet, Archives of Manitoba — https://www.gov.mb.ca/chc/archives/_assets/docs/hbca/biographical/s/spence_james-sr1773-1795.pdf (index: https://www.gov.mb.ca/chc/archives/hbca/biographical/index.html)
- **Positions (from sheet):**
  - **1773-1786: Labourer, Inland, York Factory** (refs A.16/32, fo.134; A.16/33, fo.46; A.30/1-3; A.32/1, fo.90,156; A.32/4, fo.148)
  - **1786-1795: Steersman, Inland, York Factory** (A.30/3-7)

### 3. Isaac Batt (contracted 1754; d. ca 1791) — [VERIFIED]
- **Source:** Dictionary of Canadian Biography — https://www.biographi.ca/en/bio/batt_isaac_4E.html
- **Details:** First contracted with the HBC in **1754 to serve as a labourer at York Factory** for five years at **£10 per annum**. Later inland service; was **the first HBC servant killed by Indians in the Saskatchewan area**. Had a native family as early as **1777**; left descendants in the fur-trade country.
- **OCCUPATION:** Labourer / inland trader. **NOT a hunter.** No hunting/buffalo mention in the DCB entry.
- **Note:** The HBCA biographical index also lists Batt — search "Isaac Batt" on https://www.manitoba.ca/chc/archives/hbca/biographical/index.html for his biographical sheet (not extracted this pass).

# OPEN ITEMS / GAPS (for follow-up)

1. **Occupation fields on affidavits:** The LAC *descriptions* do not state occupations. To answer the "hunter" question definitively, open the **digitized affidavit images** (each LAC file lists 3-4 digital objects) or microfilm reels (C-14933, C-14927, C-14999) and read the typed OCCUPATION line on each affidavit (David Spence, Catherine Spence, Mary Reed).
2. **George Setter scrip:** not located this pass. Search LAC "Setter, George" (father Andrew Setter). Geni page blocked by hCaptcha.
3. **Riggs scrip:** no record found for Ernest/Harmon Riggs or Mary Ann Spence; if sought, check RG15-D-II-8-c (NWT series) for Mary Ann Spence.
4. **James Spence Jr (c1780-1857)** and **George Spence** scrip records: only indirect. James Spence Jr's own scrip not captured; verify at LAC.
5. **Isaac Batt HBCA biographical sheet:** exists (HBCA index lists "Batt, Isaac") but was not extracted this pass — pull it from the HBCA biographical index.
6. **The "hunter"/buffalo-hunt claim** for any of these ancestors is **unverified** by primary records found here. If family lore asserts a buffalo-hunter ancestor, it most plausibly attaches to the Red River Métis generation (e.g. David Spence's sons / Setter sons) and must be confirmed via census occupation columns (1870/1881), scrip affidavit occupation fields, or parish records — none confirmed yet.
