> **Bottom line:** The open item "Sarah Ann Howrie's parents" is now **CLOSED**. She was **Sarah Ann HOURIE (1860–1944)**, daughter of **Philip Hourie (1833–1914)** and **Euphemia Cook Halcro (1839–1917)**. The Hourie family was an **Orkney–Métis** Red River family: patriarch **John Hourie (1779–1857)**, an Orkney HBC man from South Ronaldsay, married **Margaret Bird (1787–1847)**, a Shoshoni ("Snake") woman adopted by Chief Factor **James Curtis Bird**. The Hourie and Setter families intermarried twice (two Hourie sisters wed two Setter brothers), which is why Red River Ancestry records Roderick marrying his own "sister-in-law." The surname is of Orcadian origin in this line.

## 2. Hourie surname origins in the Red River Métis community — ✅ RESOLVED

The Red River Hourie line begins with a single **Orkney HBC man who married an Indigenous woman** — the exact ethnogenesis pattern as the parallel Spence/Setter lines already in the tree.

### The patriarch: John Hourie (1779–1857) ✅
- **Born 1779, South Ronaldsay, Orkney, Scotland** ✅ (redriverancestry HOURIE-JOHN-1779; FamilySearch LHM9-S55; WikiTree Hourie-11; Geni "John Hourie, elder"). ⚠ Note redriverancestry's own John-Jr page text inconsistently wrote "John HOURIE SR (1799-1857)" — **1779** is authoritative (1827 Red River census recorded him age 48; a 1799 birth would make him 28).
- **An Orkney man who joined the HBC for fur-trade service** ✅. redriverancestry explicitly notes: *"Unable to verify this – no such record in the online HBC Archives"* — so his HBC service sheet is not yet located ⚑; the Orkney origin and trade role are strongly attested by the site + FamilySearch but flag the HBC record as unverified.
- **Married Margaret Bird (1787–1847), c.1809** ✅ — a **Shoshoni ("Snake") Indian who had been adopted by Chief Factor James Curtis Bird (1773–1856) at Rocky Mountain House**. ⚑ The Shoshoni/"Snake" and adoption claims rest on redriverancestry + a Métis Family Resources post; treat as community/secondary, verify before presenting as primary.
- **Red River settler:** 1827 Red River census, Lot 174 (age 48, Protestant, Orkney; 5 acres; 6 cattle) ✅; 1835 census, Lot 58, St Paul Parish (8 in household, 7 acres) ✅. Died **15 Aug 1857, St Andrews Parish** ✅.
- **Children (9):** John (1810), Robert (1815), James (1818, d.young), Thomas (1820), Jane (1824, d.young), George (1825), Peter (1827), Betsy (1829), **Philip (1833)** ✅.

### The name "Hourie"
- In this Red River line the surname is **Orcadian**, brought by the Orkney patriarch John Hourie (South Ronaldsay). ✅ (redriverancestry).
- FamilySearch's Dictionary of American Family Names gives a **different, French** etymology ("Fourié"/"Fourier," Huguenot/South-African) — that explains the South African *Fourie* spelling, **not** the Red River *Hourie* line, whose bearer was Orcadian. ⚠ Do not attach the French etymology to this family.
- **Spelling variants:** Hourie / Howrie (also seen as "Henri or Hourie" in one scrip index entry, and "Fourier" in English records). Our tree's "Howrie" is the common alternate spelling. ✅

### The Houries in the wider Métis kinship network ✅
- Intermarried twice into the **Setter** line (Philip's daughters Jemima → Colin Campbell Setter 1875; Sarah Ann → Roderick McKenzie Setter 1879). ✅
- Connected to the **Bird** family (via Margaret Bird, adopted by James Curtis Bird) and through marriages into **Dennett, McKay, Sinclair, Cook, Whitford, Chastellaine, Spence** families. E.g. ## 3. Notable Houries (guides, scouts, leaders, historical connections) ✅/⚑

The Houries were a prominent, historically-active Métis family. The most famous are the **Saskatchewan branch** (descendants of Peter Hourie b.1827, who went to Touchwood Hills and married Sarah Whitford), who served on the Canadian side during the **1885 North-West Resistance**. Their direct relation to Sarah Ann's (Philip's) branch is not proven in the free sources, but they are family-name kinsmen; mark as extended-family context, not direct-line ancestry.

- **"Big Tom" Hourie (b. c.1859)** — the legendary one. **Chief Scout and interpreter for General Middleton** during the 1885 Resistance ✅ (Metis Dictionary of Biography, Vol H–K via academia.edu; saskapedia). He was **one of the three men to whom Louis Riel surrendered** (15 May 1885) ✅ (saskapedia / Canada's Military History). Famously **swam the South Saskatchewan River in running ice (March 1885) carrying dispatches** for Middleton — a celebrated feat recorded in the North-West field accounts ("How Big Tom Hourie Swam the Saskatchewan In Running Ice with Dispatches," I-Portal/USask) ✅. Described as a "Scottish mixed-blood son of Peter Hourie, General Middleton's interpreter."
- **Peter Hourie** — **General Middleton's interpreter** in 1885 ✅ (Metis Museum Native Studies Picture File, Barkwell; saskapedia). "General Middleton required the services of an interpreter and contracted Peter Hourie, a local English-speaking Metis... As a scout and guide, he was..." This is very likely the same Peter Hourie who was Sarah Ann's paternal uncle (b.1827, went to Touchwood Hills) ⚑ INFERENCE — the interpreter is documented; his identification as *the* b.1827 uncle is an inference not yet proven.
- **Robert Hourie (1815–1911)** — eldest-line brother of Philip; lived to 96; the redriverancestry forum documents his large family ✅.
- **John Hourie Jr (1835–1896)** — has his own **Lawrence Barkwell / Louis Riel Institute bio** (Metis Museum PDF), a mark of family historical importance; married Margaret McKay; their son Oliver married into the Spence/Chastellaine line ✅.
- **Audreen Hourie** — modern Métis author/editor (co-editor of Barkwell's "Métis Clothing" and contributor to the Metis Dictionary of Biography) ✅ — a contemporary prominent Hourie, though a later-generation relation.

**Sources for #3:** Metis Dictionary of Biography Vol H–K (academia.edu 38970180) ✅ · Metis Museum "Native Studies Picture File" PDF (Barkwell) ✅ · saskapedia.com (Big Tom) ✅ · I-Portal (University of Saskatchewan) "How Big Tom Hourie Swam the Saskatchewan In Running Ice with Dispatches" ✅ · redriverancestry.ca HOURIE-JOHN-1810.php + forum t=767 ✅ · Metis Museum Barkwell bio "Hourie, John (b.1835)" PDF ✅.
