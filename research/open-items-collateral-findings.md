## A. The two James Spences — ✅ VERIFIED as TWO DISTINCT people (father and son)

**Verdict: the tree is CORRECT to treat them as distinct.** They cannot be the same man on dates alone, and every primary source keeps them separate.

| | James Spence **Sr** | James Spence **Jr** (the tailor) |
|---|---|---|
| Birth | c. 1754, Birsay, Orkney ⚑ | 1778, Orphir, Orkney ⚑ (roster shows "c1782" — reconcile to **c1778**) |
| HBC role | Labourer (1773–86) → Steersman (1786–95) → in charge of Buckingham House (1793–95) | **Tailor**, hired 1797; to Red River colony 1818 |
| Death | **27 Nov 1795**, Buckingham House, age ~41 | 1857, age ~79 |
| Wife | Margaret "Nestichio" Batt (dau. of Isaac Batt) | 1st Mary (Stone/Assiniboine); 2nd **Jane Morwick** (m. 1822) |
| Father of | James Jr, Andrew, George, Margaret "Peggy" | **David Spence (1824–1885)**, Joseph, Catherine |

**Evidence:**
- ✅ **HBCA Biographical Sheet (James Spence Sr)** — https://www.gov.mb.ca/chc/archives/hbca/biographical/index.html (`spence_james-sr1773-1795.pdf`): b. ca 1754 Birsay; d. 27 Nov 1795 Buckingham House, buried 29 Nov 1795; will dated 6 Nov 1795 (proved 1796, Prerogative Court of Canterbury) names wife **Nostisho/Nestichio, mixed-blood daughter of Isaac Batt**; children **James, Andrew, George, Margareth (Peggy)**.
- ✅ **DCB — Batt, Isaac** (https://www.biographi.ca/en/bio/batt_isaac_4E.html) uses "James **Spence Sr**" explicitly and confirms the linkage: "An associate, **James Spence Sr**, left legacies to his 'Indian Wife Nestichio daughter of the deceased Isaac Batt' and their four children." The four children = James, Andrew, George, Margaret (Peggy).
- ✅ **LAC Scrip, David Spence (#1504066)** — https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/home/record?idnumber=1504066: David b. 5 Sep 1824; father **James Spence (Scottish)**; mother **Jane Morwick (Métis)**. Pins the tailor as father of David.
- ✅ **DCB — John Norquay** (https://www.biographi.ca/en/bio/norquay_john_11E.html): Norquay was raised by his paternal grandmother "**Mrs James Spence**" = **Jane Morwick**, wife of the tailor.
- ✅ **Red River Ancestry, "James Spence (1778–1857)"** — https://www.redriverancestry.ca/SPENCE-JAMES-1778.php: tailor, born Orphir 1778, hired 1797, sailed on the *King George*, to Red River 1818, m. Jane Morwick.

**Conclusion:** James Spence Sr (1754–1795) and the tailor James Spence Jr (1778–1857) are **father and son, two different men**. The tree's separation is correct and now primary-verified.

**⚠ Reconcile (minor):** roster lists Jr's birth as "c1782"; Red River Ancestry, Company Men (Morin) and the task brief say **c1780 / 1778**. Set to **c1778**. (If Sr died 1795, a son born 1778 was ~17 at his death — consistent.)

## B. Isaac Batt's wife — ✅ RESOLVED (both statements are true; they are TWO different Sarahs)

**Verdict: "Sarah Fowler" and "Sarah Lindsel" are different women — a WIFE and a MOTHER, not a conflict. The tree should record BOTH.**

- ✅ **DCB — Batt, Isaac** (https://www.biographi.ca/en/bio/batt_isaac_4E.html), opening line: "**BATT, ISAAC**, fur-trader; b. c. 1725, probably in Stanstead Abbots, Hertfordshire, England; **m. there on 18 April 1761 Sarah Fowler**; d. summer 1791 in the vicinity of Manchester House." And: "during the years 1763–66 £4 or £5 was deducted annually from his wages at his request for the support of **his wife Sarah**, of whom, however, no later mention is found."
  - **Sarah Fowler = Isaac Batt's WIFE**, an English woman he married at Stanstead Abbots before emigrating. She stayed in England; no children recorded; no later mention.
- ✅ **Wikipedia + WikiTree (Batt-208)** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Batt ; https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Batt-208): Isaac Batt was **"Son of Dantzick Batt and Sarah (Lindsel) Batt"**; he had a younger brother Dantzick.
  - **Sarah Lindsel = Isaac Batt's MOTHER** (wife of Dantzick Batt).
- **The Métis line's mother:** Isaac Batt's *native family* (the ancestor of the Spence/Setter line) is a **Cree/Indigenous woman** — the mother of **Margaret "Nestichio" Batt**, confirmed via James Spence Sr's 1795 will (DCB: "Nestichio daughter of the deceased Isaac Batt"). This Cree matriarch is a THIRD person, distinct from both Sarah Fowler and Sarah Lindsel.

**Tree correction needed:** The roster currently lists Isaac Batt's wife as "Cree matriarch (Nikawiy)" and his mother as Sarah Lindsel. **Add Sarah Fowler (m. 18 Apr 1761, Stanstead Abbots, Herts, England) as Isaac Batt's English wife**; keep the Cree matriarch as the mother of Nestichio (the Red River line); keep Sarah Lindsel as his mother (wife of Dantzick Batt). Note DCB's Sarah Fowler marriage is to the *English* wife, not the mother of the Métis line.
