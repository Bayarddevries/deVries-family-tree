David Spence, M.L.A. (1824-1885)
David Spence was a Scottish Half Breed
born on September 5, 1824, in St. John’s
parish, the son of James Spence 1 and his
second wife Jane Morwick, both Métis.
His mother was grandmother to
Manitoba’s first Premier John Norquay.
      David married Catherine Hallett on
February 15, 1844 at St. John’s. She was
the daughter of Henry Hallett and
Catherine Parenteau. David represented
St. Anne’s Parish at the Convention of
Forty. In 1877, the family moved to lot #
62 at Poplar Point. He was elected as the
first MLA from Poplar Point on
December 27, 1870. He represented
Poplar Point from 1870 to 1874 in the
Manitoba Legislative Assembly. He was
also a Justice of the Peace for Poplar
Point and Marquette. Métis scrip was
issued to David and Catherine on
October 2, 1876. She died in 1880 at age
55. David was accidentally shot in his own home by a neighbour who thought that the
rifle was unloaded. He subsequently died of his wound on September 16, 1885.
       Children of David Spence and Catherine Hallett:

       John “Jake” Spence, born August 14, 1848, he married Ellen McNab.
       Ellen Anderson Spence, born January 1, 1853, she married George Brown.
       Charles David Spence, born February 9, 1857, he married “Eliza” Foulds.
       Jane Spence, born May 26, 1859, she married William Folster.
       Mary Ann Spence, born August 8, 1861, she married Ernest Riggs.
       Harriet Spence, born June 17, 1863, she married Peter Henry Wishart.
       William Spence, born July 13, 1866, he married Martha McNab.
