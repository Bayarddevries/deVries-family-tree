## Métis assessment
- The Fleury surname is French (Norman origin via New France; e.g. François Fleury of Maskinongé, Québec).
- The family sits in the **Red River Métis parish belt** (St. François Xavier / Baie St. Paul / St. Eustache / St. Rose du Lac).
- **Métis scrip records exist for Fleurys in exactly this area**:
  - St. François Xavier, "Manitoba Affidavits" (1876 scrip): Josephte (1839), Michel (1841), Jean Baptiste (1851), Clémence (1852), Marie (1826), Patrice Joseph (1848), Marie Rose (1866), André (1862), Édouard (1865), Guillaume (1869), Agathe (1862), Louis (1820, Baie St Paul), Suzanne (1833, Baie St Paul), etc.

  - Patrice Joseph Fleury (1843–1941) of St. François Xavier — Dumont's captain at Batoche, scrip affidavit 1878 — a flagship Métis Fleury.
