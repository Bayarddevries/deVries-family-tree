## 1. David J. Riggs Jr's parents — the "David Phillip Riggs + Elizabeth Jayne" lead

### 1a. David J. Riggs Jr himself — **VERIFIED**
- b. **1804, Ontario County, New York**; d. **13 Aug 1850, Muscatine, Iowa**; buried **Oak Grove Cemetery, Muscatine Co., IA**.
- Corroboration:
  - Find A Grave memorial **#62700818** (David Riggs, 1804 Ontario Co. NY – 13 Aug 1850 Muscatine Co. IA, Oak Grove Cemetery; spouse **Catherine M Riggs 1807–1884**).
  - Rootsweb "Oak Grove Cemetery, Cedar Twp, Muscatine Co. IA" (WPA/Musser Library records): **"Riggs, David, 1803, 13 Aug 1850"** and **"Riggs, Horace M. 1824, 15 Aug 1850"** — David and his son Horace died and were buried two days apart. (Primary cemetery record.)
  - WikiTree **Riggs-7792** (David J Riggs Jr, 1804–1850, buried Oak Grove, Muscatine; Find A Grave #62700818 / FamilySearch QV27-MMBG).
- **Verdict: VERIFIED.** The identity (Ontario Co. NY → Muscatine IA, d. 1850) is solid across 3 independent free sources.

### 1b. The parents lead (David Phillip Riggs + Elizabeth Jayne) — **INFERENCE, NOT verified**
- **Claim (single source — FamilySearch tree):** father **David Phillip Riggs (1771–Deceased)** [FS G32Q-T49], mother **Elizabeth Jayne (1772–1837)** [FS LC7H-FNZ]; parents married **1788**; siblings Rhoda (1789–1832), Philip (1798–1878), David Jr (1804–1850), Daniel (1809–1849).
- **Contradiction / non-corroboration:**
  - **WikiTree Riggs-7792 explicitly lists David Jr's father and mother as UNKNOWN** ("[father unknown]", "[mother unknown]").
  - **Find A Grave #62700818 lists NO parents** (spouse + children only).
  - No independent secondary source names these parents.
- **Verdict:** The parents lead is **single-source (FamilySearch tree data only) → INFERENCE.** It is the *best available* candidate (no alternative offered anywhere), and it is internally chronologically consistent (father b.1771 → age 33 at David Jr's 1804 birth; marriage 1788 → children 1789–1809). But **do not present it as fact** in the tree without a primary NY record (birth/baptism/census or a FamilySearch *record* attached to it, not just tree links).

### 1c. Note on David Jr's spouse and dates (minor inconsistencies to be aware of)
- FamilySearch lists his wife as "Catherine M **Hendricks Hilbarth Kanann**" (1807–1864) — the multiple surnames are a FamilySearch-tree conflation (she may have been remarried and/or the tree merged more than one wife). Find A Grave gives **Catherine M Riggs 1807–1884**. WikiTree (Hendricks-6344) has her as **Catherine M (Hendricks) Kanann**, married *about 1824 in Indiana* (uncertain). **Death year conflicts: 1864 (FS) vs 1884 (FG)** — flag, do not fix blindly.
- Earlier research flagged a WSGS newsletter item: a "David Riggs" married "Catherine Hendricks" on **12 Jan 1861, Hixton, Jackson Co., WI**. That **cannot** be this David Jr (d. 1850) — it is a *different* David Riggs (likely a son or a later-generation relative of the same Jackson Co., WI cluster). Do not conflate.

## 2. Riggs surname origins & pre–Ontario NY geography

### 2a. Surname origin — **VERIFIED (etymology)**
- **English (Dorset)** topographic surname. Per *Dictionary of American Family Names* (Hanks, 2003): a variant of **Ricks** (with voicing of /ks/ to /gs/) OR a variant of **Rigg** with post-medieval excrescent **-s**. It derives from Old English **"hrycg"** / Middle English **"rigge"** = *ridge* (a person who lived on/by a ridge). (Corroborated by FamilySearch surname, Ancestry surname page, SurnameDB, HouseOfNames.)
- **NOT specifically Quaker.** A few early English Riggs were Quakers (e.g., Ambrose Rigg, 1635–1705, of Westmorland), but **there is no evidence this particular NY/Ohio/Iowa/Wisconsin/Manitoba line was Quaker.** Do not claim Quaker heritage without a source.

### 2b. Where the family was before Ontario NY — **INFERENCE (FamilySearch tree only)**
FamilySearch tree extends the line (all **INFERENCE** — no independent corroboration found):
- **David Riggs Sr (1709–1799)** + **Elizabeth Cox (1716–1799)** — FS tree.
- → **Philip Riggs (1744–1821)**, b. **Basking Ridge, Bernards Twp, Somerset, New Jersey**; married **Mary "Polly" Pierce (1748–1796)** before 1776 in **Pennsylvania**; d. 1821, **buried Benton, Yates Co., New York** [FS KN8G-YVH]. Children include **David Phillip Riggs (1771)**.
- → **David Phillip Riggs (1771)** + **Elizabeth Jayne** (see §1b) → David J. Riggs Jr (b. 1804, Ontario Co., NY).
- **Narrative:** the family was in **New Jersey** (Somerset Co.) by the 1740s, passed through **Pennsylvania**, and had settled in **western/central New York (Yates + Ontario counties)** by the late 1700s — *before* David Jr's 1804 birth in Ontario County. So "Ontario, NY" is the starting point **only within the verified span**; the deeper US roots appear to be New Jersey/England.
- **Caution / important distinction:** a separate, well-documented **"Edward Riggs of Roxbury, Massachusetts"** line (d. 1671; genealogy in the alvyray.com PDF and Burr Cook's page) is a **different** New England family. **Do not connect this line to the Edward-Riggs-of-Roxbury family** — no source links them.
- **Verdict:** The pre-1804 chain (David Riggs Sr 1709 → Philip 1744 → David Phillip 1771) is **entirely FamilySearch tree data → INFERENCE.** Useful as a lead/plausible migration narrative, but unconfirmed. Highest-value primary check: a **Yates/Ontario Co., NY record** for David Phillip Riggs or Philip Riggs (the 1800/1810/1820 censuses and Yates Co. burials are the strongest targets).

## 4. US → Red River migration story — **VERIFIED in the Ernest generation**

### 4a. The verified waypoints (each documented)
1. **Ohio by 1834:** Harmon b. 27 Aug 1834 in **Mill Creek Twp, Hamilton Co., Ohio** (FamilySearch K2WH-FGM) — i.e., the family had left NY by the 1830s.
2. **Iowa by 1850:** Harmon is recorded in the **1850 US Census in the household of "Catharine Riggs"** (his mother, then a widow) at **Seventy-Six Twp, Muscatine, Iowa** (FamilySearch source). David Jr died 13 Aug 1850 in Muscatine (Find A Grave #62700818; Rootsweb WPA listing).
3. **Wisconsin by 1858:** Harmon m. Amelia M. Williams **11 Nov 1858, Jackson Co., WI** (FamilySearch; WeRelate); lived **Alma, Jackson Co., WI** ~10 years; d. **27 Oct 1874, Alma Center, Jackson Co., WI** (FamilySearch).
4. **Manitoba by 1887:** Harmon's son **Ernest Charles Riggs (1859/60–1932)**, born in **Wisconsin**, migrated north; married **Mary Ann Spence on 1887 in Portage la Prairie, Manitoba** (redriverancestry SPENCE-DAVID-1834). Ernest appears in the **Canada Census 1911, Macdonald RM, MB** (FamilySearch LC83-8ZF, via son Ray Howard Riggs's record). Ernest d. **1932, Portage la Prairie**; Mary Ann (Spence) Riggs d. **29 May 1932, Portage la Prairie** (WikiTree Spence-6030; Find A Grave via Eva Myrtle Riggs Brooks memorial).

### 4b. Interpretation
- The **US→Red River migration is VERIFIED**, but it happened in **Ernest's generation** (c. 1880s), not Harmon's. Harmon lived and died in **Wisconsin** (1874).
- **Ernest was the one who crossed into Manitoba** (Red River / Portage la Prairie district), marrying into the long-established Spence (Métis) family there in 1887. His **two brothers, Ervin Payne Riggs (1861–1939) and Murray Hezekiah Riggs (1868–1946)** (FamilySearch) appear to have **remained in the US** — i.e., the Red River branch came through Ernest alone.
- **Timing/motive (INFERENCE):** Ernest's migration (1880s) into the Portage la Prairie area is consistent with the **post-1870 Manitoba settlement / Half-breed scrip era**, when the Canadian government opened former Hudson's Bay Company lands and both Métis and newcomers took up land around Portage la Prairie. The specific reason Ernest came is unknown from free sources — **inference only**.
- **Minor date inconsistencies to flag:** Ernest 1859/60 (FS 1860–1932; FG/Eva memorial 1859–1932); Mary Ann Spence 1861/1863–1930/1932 (redriverancestry 1861; FG 1863–1930; WikiTree 1861–1932, d. 29 May 1932). Use consistent "1859/60–1932" for Ernest and "1861/63–1930/32" for Mary Ann pending primary vital stats.
