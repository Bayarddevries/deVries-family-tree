# Parenteau Line — Findings (FINAL)

Research task: Find Catherine Parenteau's parents; expand the Parenteau line.
Target: **Catherine Parenteau** (c.1799–1857, also 1806) m. **Henry Hallett Jr.** 18 Oct 1824,
St. John's, Red River Settlement; mother of **Catherine Hallett** (1824–1880, m. David Spence).

File written 2026-08-13 by Hermes research subagent (free sources only).

---

## ANSWER: Catherine Parenteau's father = JEAN BAPTISTE PARENTEAU (from Quebec)

The Ancestry-tree lead "**Joseph V. Parenteau (1752–1817) + Suzanne Cree**" as Catherine's parents
is **INCORRECT / UNSUPPORTED**. Free, reputable secondary sources consistently name Catherine's
father as **Jean Baptiste Parenteau**, and the "Joseph Victor Parenteau + Suzanne Cree" couple is a
**separate** Parenteau family. Details below.

---

## VERIFIED FINDINGS

### V1. Catherine Parenteau was the mother of Catherine (Hallett) Spence — VERIFIED (primary record)
- Library & Archives Canada (BAC-LAC), search/collection notice idnumber=1504062:
  "Scrip affidavit for Spence, Catherine; born: 1824; husband: David Spence; **father: Henry Hallett
  (Métis); mother: Catherine Parato (Métis)**."
  URL: https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/fra/accueil/notice?idnumber=1504062
- Confirms the target line (Catherine Parenteau → Catherine Hallett → Spence). "Parato" = Parenteau
  as rendered by the clerk. Does NOT name Catherine's own parents.

### V2. Catherine Parenteau married Henry Hallett Jr. 18 Oct 1824 — VERIFIED (multiple sources)
- Metis Museum / Barkwell, "Palliser Expedition 1857" PDF: "Henry married Catherine Parenteau
  (born 1799-1804) on October 18, 1824 at St. [John's]."
  URL: https://www.metismuseum.ca/media/document.php/13789.Palliser%20Expedition%201857.pdf
- Archive.org, "History And Genealogy Of Li Exovedes Du Branche Sud" (biographies): "He married
  Catherine Parenteau on 18 October 1824 at St-John's, RRS."
  URL: https://archive.org/stream/history-and-genealogy-of-li-exovedes-du-branche-sud/

### V3. Catherine's father = JEAN BAPTISTE PARENTEAU (from Quebec) — VERIFIED (2 independent secondary sources)
- **Red River Ancestry — "Henry Hallett (1773-1844)"** (page confirmed in full):
  "On Oct 18, 1824 at St Johns Church the following children were baptized: Henry Jr. and his wife
  Catherine, James, Anne, William and Elizabeth. **Son Henry also married Catherine PARENTEAU that
  day, daughter of Jean Baptiste PARENTEAU from Quebec.**"
  URL: https://www.redriverancestry.ca/HALLETT-HENRY-1773.php
- **Lawrence Barkwell, "Metis Dictionary of Biography, Volume H to K"** (Academia.edu):
  "[Henry Hallett Jr.] was the son of Henry Hallett Sr. and an unnamed Indian woman. He married
  Catherine Parenteau the **daughter of Jean Baptiste Parenteau** in 1824 at Red River."
  URL: https://www.academia.edu/38970180/Metis_Dictionary_of_Biography_Volume_H_to_K
- These two independent sources agree: **Jean Baptiste Parenteau, a man from Quebec**, was
  Catherine Parenteau's father.

## CORRECTION of the Lead (Joseph V. Parenteau + Suzanne Cree)

The documented "Joseph V(ictor) Parenteau + Suzanne Cree" couple is **a different family** — not
Catherine's parents:
- **MHS Manitoba — Memorable Manitobans: Pierre Parenteau (1817-1893)** (page confirmed in full):
  "Born at the Red River Settlement in 1817, **son of Joseph Victor Parenteau (1776-1822) and
  Suzanne "Cree" Richard (c1780-1830)**," — Pierre was a Métis leader, delegate to the Convention of
  Forty, and Chairman of Riel's Council (Exovedate) in 1885; died Batoche 14 Apr 1893.
  URL: http://www.mhs.mb.ca/docs/people/parenteau_p.shtml
- Notes:
  - The lead's dates (1752-1817) are wrong vs. the documented (1776-1822).
  - The mother's documented surname is **Richard**; "Cree" is her ethnic nickname, not a surname.
  - Therefore an Ancestry tree conflated the Joseph Victor Parenteau + Suzanne "Cree" (Richard)
    family with Catherine's parentage. **Reject this lead for Catherine.**

## EXPANSION of the Parenteau LINE

### A. The Jean-Baptiste-Parenteau line (Catherine's father) — identified only, not fully expanded
- Identity established as "Jean Baptiste Parenteau from Quebec" (Red River Ancestry; Barkwell).
- NOT expanded deeper in this pass: multiple men named Jean-Baptiste Parenteau appear in
  Quebec/Red River records, and free sources do not clearly isolate which one was Catherine's
  father. (No baptismal/parish record naming Catherine's own parents surfaced in free searches.)

### B. The Joseph Victor Parenteau (1776-1822) + Suzanne "Cree" Richard (c1780-1830) line — better documented
- Parents of **Pierre Parenteau (1817-1893)** (MHS).
- Batoche/Duck Lake/Onion Lake descendants appear in the Metis National Historical Online Database:
  - **Métis National Historical Online Database**, search family_name=PARENTEAU returns **512 records**
    (mostly **Northwest Scrip** at Duck Lake, Batoche, Onion Lake, Prince Albert, Saskatoon):
    names include Pierre (1879), Jean Baptiste (1883, 1885), Joseph, Louis, Julie (1870),
    Celina (1873), Damase (1877), Alexandre (1874), etc.
    URL: https://www.metisnationdatabase.ca/results.php?source=All&family_name=PARENTEAU
  - These are the Batoche-area Parenteau scrip claimants — consistent with Pierre Parenteau's
    documented move to Batoche (MHS).
- Corroborating note: Facebook Metis Family Resources genealogy group posts (secondary) cite a line
  "Joseph Victor Parenteau --> Joseph Dodet Parenteau + Angelique Godon --> Jean Baptiste Parenteau
  + Pelagie Dumont --> ...", and "Petit Louis" / "Joseph / Dode" Parenteau naming in scrip records.
  (Not relied on for verification; included for context.)
