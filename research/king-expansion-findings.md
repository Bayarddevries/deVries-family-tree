### King surname origin (VERIFIED — surname dictionaries; national origin of THIS line = INFERENCE)
- **King** is English/Scottish: a nickname from Middle English/Old English *cyning*/*cyng* "king" — given to one with kingly bearing, or someone who played the part of a king in pageants/tournaments/folk ritual. In North America it has also absorbed German **König/Küng**, French **Roy**, Slavic **Kralj/Krol**, and appears as an artificial Jewish surname. **Irish** "King" is commonly an anglicization of Irish names containing *rí* ("king").
  - Source: FamilySearch Name Meaning (Dictionary of American Family Names © Patrick Hanks 2003, 2006), on Thomas Allan King profile; also https://www.findmypast.co.uk/surname/king ; https://www.ancestry.com/last-name-meaning/king (free surname-meaning page, not a record).
- **SD&G settlement context:** Stormont, Dundas & Glengarry was first settled by **United Empire Loyalists**, then heavily by **Scottish Highlanders** (many Roman Catholic, esp. Glengarry) and **Irish** settlers.
  - Source: https://toosquaretobehip.com/the-united-counties-of-stormont-dundas-and-glengarry-tracing-back-loyalists-and-irish-roots/
- **INFERENCE:** The "Scottish/Irish" ethnic label attached to this King line is plausible and consistent with SD&G's Loyalist/Scottish/Irish settlement, and the King surname is documented as English/Scottish/Irish. BUT the specific national origin of THIS William King (1817) is NOT yet proven from a primary record (no ship record/naturalization/1842–1871 census birthplace located in this pass). Treat "Scottish or Irish" as unconfirmed.
