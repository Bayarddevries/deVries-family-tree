# Deeper Ancestor Lines — Verification Findings

**Date researched:** 13 Aug 2026
**Method:** Free/primary sources only (Red River Ancestry, FamilySearch free, Manitoba Historical Society, Exovedes genealogy text, WeRelate, WSGS newsletter, RootsWeb). No paywalled Ancestry used.
**Status key:**
- **VERIFIED** = corroborated by a free genealogical source that cites primary records (baptisms, church marriages, scrip affidavits, censuses).
- **PARTIAL** = names confirmed but dates/relationships conflict or are weak.
- **INFERENCE** = appears only in the shared Ancestry DNA-app screenshot; no independent corroboration, and in several cases contradicted.

**Headline:** Three of the four "deeper" lines have *errors inherited from the Ancestry app*. The most likely-correct mothers/wives differ from what the app shows, and the Parenteau and Riggs lines are weaker / likely conflated.

---

## 1. MORWICK line — Jane Morwick

| Element | Ancestry-app screenshot | Verified findings | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Father | James Morwick (b.1775, d.1817) | **James Morwick, born c.1778 Kirkwall, Orkney; died 28 Oct 1865, Red River** | PARTIAL — dates off |
| Mother | Catherine Dungas | **Sarah Sabiston (1800–1872)** | **CONFLICT** — see below |
| Jane Morwick | daughter | born c.1819, St Andrews Parish; m. James Setter 27 Dec 1836 at Old St Andrews Church | VERIFIED |

**Findings (Red River Ancestry, "James Morwick (1778–1865)"; "James Setter (1810–1857)"):**
- Jane Morwick (b. c.1819) is recorded as the daughter of **James Morwick and Sarah Sabiston**. Her birth is tied to Sarah's scrip affidavit. She married James Setter on 27 Dec 1836.
- James Morwick was born c.1778 (Kirkwall, Orkney), son of an Orkney woman and **James Morwick Sr (c.1753, Orkney)**. James Morwick died 28 Oct 1865 in Red River. Sarah Sabiston died 13 Jan 1872, Poplar Point.
- **"Catherine Dungas" as James Morwick's wife is almost certainly an ERROR / conflation.** Catherine Dungas/Dunges/Dansee is the documented *fourth wife of Henry Hallett* (see §3), not a Morwick. No free source connects a Catherine Dungas to the Morwick family.

**Parents (of James Morwick):** James Morwick Sr (b. c.1753, Orkney) + an unnamed Orkney woman. (INFERENCE per RRA; little further detail.)

**Recommended correction:** Jane Morwick's parents should read **James Morwick (c.1778–1865) + Sarah Sabiston (1800–1872)**. Drop "Catherine Dungas."

## 2. PARENTEAU line — Catherine Parenteau (wife of Henry Hallett Jr)

| Element | Ancestry-app screenshot | Verified findings | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Father | Joseph V. Parenteau (1752–1817) | Joseph Victor Parenteau **1776–1822** | **CONFLICT** — dates off |
| Mother | Suzanne Cree | **Suzanne "Cree" Richard (c.1780–1830)** | PARTIAL |
| Catherine Parenteau | daughter | born **1804** (Exovedes); married Henry Hallett Jr **18 Oct 1824** at St John's, Red River | PARTIAL |

**Findings:**
- Manitoba Historical Society (Memorable Manitobans, "Pierre Parenteau 1817–1893"): Pierre Parenteau, Metis leader, b. 1817 Red River, **son of Joseph Victor Parenteau (1776–1822) and Suzanne "Cree" Richard (c.1780–1830)**. → This couple is VERIFIED as parents of *Pierre* Parenteau. Date conflict with app: **1776–1822 (MHS) vs 1752–1817 (app)**.
- **KEY UNRESOLVED POINT:** Whether Joseph Victor Parenteau + Suzanne Richard are also the parents of *Catherine* Parenteau (b.1804, wife of Henry Hallett Jr) is **NOT confirmed.** Red River Ancestry states Catherine Parenteau, married to Henry Hallett Jr in 1824, was **"daughter of Jean Baptiste Parenteau from Quebec"** — a *different* Parenteau line. A Facebook Metis Family Resources thread explicitly warns that linking this Catherine to "Joseph Victor Parenteau and Suzanne Cree" is **incorrect**.
- WikiTree's Catherine (Parenteau) Hallett profile (b. abt.1799–abt.1857) lists **father unknown / mother unknown**, corroborating that her parentage is unproven.

**Recommended correction:** Flag as **UNVERIFIED / likely conflated.** The app's parents (Joseph V. Parenteau + Suzanne Cree) are a real couple (parents of Pierre), but their being parents of *Catherine* Parenteau is disputed. Treat Catherine Parenteau's parents as **unknown** pending a record naming them; note the RRA "Jean Baptiste Parenteau" lead.

## 3. HALLETT line — Henry Hallett Jr

| Element | Ancestry-app screenshot | Verified findings | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Father | Henry Hallett (1773–1844) | **Henry Hallett Sr, bapt. 15 Apr 1773, Battersea, Surrey; d. 9 Mar 1844, St John's, Red River** | **VERIFIED** (dates match) |
| Mother | Catherine T. Tenance | **Catherine Crise (Cree), first wife, d. c.1800** | **CONFLICT** — see below |
| Henry Hallett Jr | son | born **1798/1799**, Buckingham House; m. Catherine Parenteau 18 Oct 1824 | VERIFIED (birth year 1798/99) |

**Findings:**
- **Henry Hallett Sr is VERIFIED:** baptized 15 Apr 1773, St Mary's, Battersea, Surrey (primary baptism record via RRA & FamilySearch); died 9 Mar 1844, St John's Anglican, Red River; buried St John's cemetery. Born to **Thomas Hallett (1734–1781) and Catherine Turnor** (FamilySearch) / "Catherine" (RRA). His paternal grandfather: **Thomas Hallett Sr (bapt. 21 Sep 1710, Battersea)**. (Henry's aunt Elizabeth Hallett married HBC surveyor Philip Turnor.)
- Henry Sr had **four documented wives**: (1) **Catherine Crise/Cree (d. c.1800)**, (2) Marie (mixed-blood, d. c.1807), (3) **Catherine Tenanse**, (4) **Catherine Dungas/Dunges/Dansee**.
- **Henry Hallett Jr was the son of the FIRST wife, Catherine Crise (Cree).** The Exovedes genealogy ("Li Exovedes du Branche Sud") states: *"Catherine Parenteau ... married Henry Hallett Jr., son of Sir Henry Hallett and Catherine Crise, on 18 October 1824."* RRA likewise shows Henry Jr born 1799 under the first wife; Catherine Tenanse is the mother of daughter **Ann (b. 31 May 1809)** — a later child.
- **→ The app's "Catherine T. Tenance" as mother of Henry Jr is an INFERENCE/error.** Correct mother = **Catherine Crise (Cree)**. (Note: "Catherine Tenanse" and "Catherine Dungas" are two separate later wives; FamilySearch's tree has itself conflated a "Catherine Dansee Cree" — treat FS spouse names here as unreliable.)

**Recommended correction:** Henry Hallett Jr's parents = **Henry Hallett Sr (1773–1844) + Catherine Crise (Cree)**.

## 4. RIGGS line — Harmon M. Riggs / David J. Riggs Jr

| Element | Ancestry-app screenshot | Free-source finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harmon M. Riggs + Amelia Williams | married | **Amelia Williams (b. abt. 1841, Pennsylvania) married Harmon Riggs 11 Nov 1858, Jackson Co., Wisconsin** | PARTIAL — marriage matches |
| Amelia Williams's parents | — | **Irad D. Williams (b. abt. 1804) + Eunice Lakin** (WeRelate) | INFERENCE (user-tree) |
| David J. Riggs Jr (1804–1850) + Catherine Hendricks | married | A **David Riggs** married **Catherine Hendricks 12 Jan 1861, Hixton, Jackson Co., WI** (Wisconsin State Genealogical Society newsletter) | **CONFLICT** — dates |

**Findings:**
- **This is a separate, likely American (non-Métis) line**, geographically anchored in **Jackson County, Wisconsin**, not Red River.
- The only free corroboration of "Harmon Riggs + Amelia Williams" is a WeRelate user-tree entry: Amelia Williams b. abt.1841 Pennsylvania, m. Harmon Riggs 11 Nov 1858, Jackson, WI; her parents Irad D. Williams + Eunice Lakin. **User-tree, low confidence.**
- The WSGS newsletter lists a "David Riggs" married "Catherine Hendricks" on **12 Jan 1861** at Hixton (Jackson Co., WI). If that marriage belongs to this David Riggs, then the app's dates (b.1804, d.1850) are **inconsistent** — he could not marry in 1861 if he died in 1850. **Date conflict flagged.**
- No free primary record confirms David Riggs's 1804 birth, 1850 death, or Harmon Riggs's vital dates. This line is **poorly documented in free sources** and should be treated as **UNVERIFIED / INFERENCE** pending better records (likely US census/marriage records for Jackson Co., WI).

**Recommended correction:** Keep this line flagged as **unverified**. Verify via US federal census + Jackson Co., WI marriage registers before accepting. It does not belong to the Red River Métis heritage cluster.

## Actionable corrections for the family tree
1. Morwick: set Jane Morwick's mother to **Sarah Sabiston**; father James Morwick (c.1778–1865).
2. Hallett: set Henry Hallett Jr's mother to **Catherine Crise (Cree)**; keep Henry Sr (1773–1844).
3. Parenteau: leave Catherine Parenteau's parents **blank/unknown** (or note the "Jean Baptiste Parenteau from Quebec" lead from RRA); do **not** attach Joseph Victor Parenteau + Suzanne Richard to her without a primary record.
4. Riggs: keep **unverified**, flag for US census/marriage-record follow-up; not part of the Red River Métis cluster.
