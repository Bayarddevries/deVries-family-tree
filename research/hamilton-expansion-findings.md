## 1. John James Hamilton's parents — ✅ RESOLVED (was OPEN)

**Verdict: John James Hamilton (1856–1913) was the son of Joseph Hamilton (1821–1889) and Mary Busby (1831–1921).** Confirmed directly on the free FamilySearch ancestor page (brief-life-history + "Parents and Siblings" section, which renders without login) and corroborated by the 1871 Canada census (son in Joseph Hamilton's household), an 1848 Ontario newspaper marriage notice, and a Find a Grave burial.

### Joseph Hamilton (1821–1889) — John James's father ✅
- **Born:** 22 Dec 1821, **Glenedagh, Kilfian, County Mayo, Ireland** (FamilySearch LDRS-H5F ✅; WikiTree Hamilton-30915 corroboration ⚑).
- **Died:** 20 Feb 1889, **Neepawa, Manitoba**; buried **Riverside Cemetery, Neepawa, MB** (Find a Grave #228283742) ✅ — i.e. the immigrant's son carried the family to western Manitoba.
- **Parents (John James's grandparents):** **John Hamilton (1791–1857)** + **Eleanor Jane Preston (1798–1884)** — the Irish immigrant generation (FamilySearch "Parents and Siblings" on LDRS-H5F) ✅.
- **Spouse:** Mary Busby, **m. 24 Feb 1848, Mornington, Perth Co., Ontario** (FamilySearch LDRS-H5F ✅). **Corroborated by an Ontario newspaper marriage notice:** *Christian Guardian*, Toronto, 19 Apr 1848 — "In Mornington, Feb 24th, Joseph Hamilton & Mary Busby" (from Ontario Marriage Notices/Wilson, cited in the "Uniting Our Efforts" family history) ✅.
- **Residences:** Mornington, Perth, Ontario (1851); **Elderslie, Bruce County, Ontario (1871)** — so the family moved from Perth to Bruce County while still in Ontario. ✅
- **Children (13; 9 sons + 4 daughters):** Jonathan Joseph (1850–1928), Sophia Ellen (1852–1913), Robert (1853–), Jemima Jane (1854–1872), Abner (1855–), **John James (1856–1913)**, Robinson Crusoe (1860–1943), Abner (1863–1948), Ida Ann (1865–), Richard (1869–), Mary (1870–), Charles William (1871–1902), Edmund Francis (1877–1961). ✅

### Mary Busby (1831–1921) — John James's mother ✅
- **Born/Died:** 1831–1921. **Confirmed as John James's mother** (FamilySearch KZZQ-FKD: "his father, Joseph Hamilton, was 34 and his mother, Mary Busby, was 24" at his 1856 birth — consistent). ✅
- **Marriage:** to Joseph Hamilton, 24 Feb 1848, Mornington, Perth Co., Ontario (newspaper + FamilySearch) ✅.
- *Her own parents ⛔ NOT-FOUND in free sources this pass* (Busby family, Perth Co., Ontario; gated on FamilySearch person page). **Open item.**

### Hamilton origins BEFORE Ontario — ⚠ IMPORTANT CORRECTION to prior pass
- The earlier pass inferred "Scottish settler line." That is now **revised**: this specific Hamilton line is **IRISH, from County Mayo, Ireland**.
  - Joseph Hamilton (the first generation to emigrate to Canada) was **born in Glenedagh, Co. Mayo, Ireland (1821)** ✅ (FamilySearch; corroborated by WikiTree).
  - His parents, **John Hamilton (1791–1857)** & **Eleanor Jane Preston (1798–1884)**, are the immigrant generation (John born in Ireland c.1791, emigrated with his family to Upper Canada/Ontario) ✅.
- **Why the surname is "Hamilton":** the name is of **Scottish** origin (habitational from Hamilton, Lanarkshire, named after the English/Scottish Hamiltons; Dictionary of American Family Names). But by the 17th century Hamiltons had settled heavily in **northern Ireland**, and this branch is specifically a **County Mayo, Ireland** family. So: *Scottish-origin surname, but this line's immediate roots are Irish (Co. Mayo).* ✅ (FamilySearch name-meaning note.)
- **Tree action:** re-tag the Hamilton line as **Irish (County Mayo) settler**, not "Scottish." (The Buchanan line below IS Scottish-origin through Isabella Watson, and Irish through John Buchanan.)

## 2. Jane Buchanan's family — ✅ RESOLVED (was partially open)

**Verdict: Jane Buchanan (1859–1931) was the daughter of John Buchanan (1829/1831–1909) and Isabella Watson (1837–1917).** Confirmed on her free Find a Grave memorial (parents listed) and her parents' memorials; corroborated by a 1909 Neepawa Press obituary.

### John Buchanan (1829/1831–1909) — Jane's father ✅
- **Born:** memorial 10 Aug 1829 ⚠ / family bio 1 Nov 1831 (discrepancy to reconcile) — **Omagh, County Tyrone, Northern Ireland**. Came to Canada in 1847 at age ~17 with his parents and siblings. ✅
- **Died:** 15 Feb 1909, **Langford, Western Manitoba (Beautiful Plains/Neepawa area)**; buried **Rosedale (Buchanan) Cemetery, R.M. of Rosedale, Neepawa** — Find a Grave #132595827 ✅.
- **Parents (Jane's grandparents):** **Andrew Buchanan Sr (1790–1847)** + **Jane Young Long (McNeilands) Buchanan (1794–1861)** ✅.
- **Origin:** Irish (Ulster) — Co. Tyrone. **NOT Métis.** ✅
- **Family bio (from Find a Grave #132595827, contributor "DP"):** 7th son of Andrew & Jane; siblings Robert, Charles, James, William, Andrew, Samuel, and only sister Jane; settled in **Elma, County Perth, Ontario**; married Isabella Watson ~1855; later homesteaded near Neepawa, MB. **Neepawa Press obituary (16 Feb 1909):** "John Buchanan, one of the early settlers here… died… at the residence of his son-in-law Dan McGillivray… came from Elma, County of Perth, Ont. He is survived by 7 children — Mrs. J. Hamilton; Mrs. Keating; Mrs. W. Ash; Mrs. Dan McGillivray; Robert; James, & William." ✅
- **Note:** This is the *father of Jane*, the *father-in-law* of John James Hamilton. A copy of his **Last Will and Testament** (probated Portage la Prairie, Manitoba) is even attached as a FamilySearch story on John James's page — corroborating the family connection and the Manitoba settlement. ✅

- **Origin:** Scottish-settler line (born in Ontario). **NOT Métis.** ✅

## 3. Corroboration: Ontario → Manitoba → Saskatchewan migration — ✅ VERIFIED

The path was previously reconstructed from births/deaths. It is now confirmed **independently on both the Hamilton and Buchanan sides** with place-level primary sources:

| Generation | Ontario | Manitoba | Saskatchewan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joseph Hamilton (father) | b. Mayo Ireland; m. Mornington, Perth 1848; Mornington (1851), Elderslie/Bruce (1871) | **died Neepawa, MB 1889** ✅ | — |
| John James Hamilton (son) | b. Mornington, Perth 1856 | **Macdonald RM, MB (1901 census)** ✅ | **Moose Jaw, SK (1911)**; died **Tisdale, SK 1913** ✅ |
| John Buchanan (father-in-law) | Elma, Perth Co., Ont. | **died Langford, MB 1909** ✅ | — |
| Jane Buchanan Hamilton (daughter-in-law) | b. Elma Twp, Perth 1859 | (settled Beautiful Plains MB) | died Tisdale, SK 1931 ✅ |
| Guy Wentworth Hamilton (grandson) | — | **b. Beautiful Plains MB 1882** ✅ | Prince Albert (1911), Melfort, **died Tisdale SK 1946** ✅ |

**Key detail:** Joseph Hamilton (the Co. Mayo immigrant's son) and John Buchanan both **died in the Neepawa/Beautiful Plains region of western Manitoba** (1889 and 1909). This is the SAME Beautiful Plains district where Guy Hamilton was born in 1882 — i.e. the two families (Hamilton and Buchanan) both settled Beautiful Plains, intermarried (John James ⚭ Jane Buchanan, c.1880), then the next generation moved on to Saskatchewan (Moose Jaw, Prince Albert, Melfort, Tisdale). This is strong, source-backed corroboration of the full chain.
