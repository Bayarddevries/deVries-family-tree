## 4. Jemima Hourie + Colin Campbell Setter — family (the NEW branch)

Jemima Hourie (b. **12 Oct 1857, St. Andrews/Old Kildonan, MB**; d. **Nov 1950**, Portage la Prairie, age 93), daughter of **Philip Hourie (1833–1914) ⚭ Euphemia Cook Halcro (1839–1917)**. Jemima is **Sarah Ann Hourie's older sister** — two Hourie sisters married two Setter brothers (Colin and Roderick). She married **Colin Campbell Setter (1854–1935)**, Deputy Sheriff for the district. Marriage date: **22 Jul 1875** ⚑ WikiTree (obit says 1877 ⚠ minor conflict). They farmed **High Bluff, MB**, later moved to **St. Marks** (St. Laurent area). Buried Hillside Cemetery, Portage la Prairie.
