## 1. RIGGS LINE

### Chain (bottom-up as it enters the tree)
```
David J. Riggs Jr (1804–1850) + Catherine M. Hendricks
  └─ Harmon Miles Riggs (1834–1874) + Amelia Williams (m. 11 Nov 1858)
       └─ Ernest Charles Riggs (b. ~1859) + Mary Ann Spence (1861–1932) [m. 1887 Portage la Prairie]
            └─ Ella Alberta Riggs + Alan Setter  →  Doris Setter  →  ... → Bayard
```

### 1a. Ernest Charles Riggs ⚭ Mary Ann Spence — **VERIFIED**
- redriverancestry `SPENCE-DAVID-1834.php`: "In 1887 daughter Mary Ann married Ernest Charles RIGGS in Portage la Prairie, **son of Amelia WILLIAMS and Harmon Miles RIGGS (1834-1874)**."
- WikiTree `Spence-6030`: Mary Ann (Spence) Riggs (1861–1932), husband **Ernest Charles Riggs** (Riggs-6115).
- FamilySearch `LC83-8ZF` (Ray Howard Riggs, 1897–1964): "Ray Riggs in household of Ernest Riggs, Canada Census 1911" (Macdonald RM, MB) — confirms Ernest's household post-1900.
- **Verdict:** The Ernest–Mary Ann marriage and Ernest's parentage (Amelia Williams + Harmon Miles Riggs) are solid.

### 1b. Harmon Miles Riggs (1834–1874) ⚭ Amelia Williams — **VERIFIED** (parents of Ernest; marriage record-level for Amelia)
- Parents-of-Ernest: confirmed by redriverancestry (above).
- Marriage: WeRelate `Family:Harmon_Riggs_and_Amelia_Williams_(1)` + `Person:Amelia_Williams_(14)`: **Harmon Riggs married Amelia Williams, 11 Nov 1858, Jackson, Wisconsin, USA.** Amelia b. abt 1841, Pennsylvania; **parents = Irad D. Williams (abt 1804) + Eunice Lakin** (WeRelate tree — INFERENCE).
- Chronology fits: Harmon b. 1834 + m. 1858 → Ernest b. ~1859 (consistent with roster.md "Ernest Charles Riggs (1859)").
- **Verdict:** Harmon–Amelia confirmed; Amelia's own parents (Irad Williams + Eunice Lakin) = INFERENCE (single WeRelate tree).

### 1c. Harmon Miles Riggs = son of David J. Riggs Jr + Catherine M. Hendricks — **VERIFIED** (cross-source), parents of David Jr = INFERENCE
- FamilySearch person page `KCZR-38G` "David J Riggs Jr (1804–1850)": born 1804 in **Ontario, New York, US**; father **David Phillip Riggs** (age 33 at David Jr's birth → b. ~1771), mother **Elizabeth Jayne**; "He had at least 4 sons and 7 daughters with **Catherine M Hendricks** (alt. Hendricks Hilbarth Kanann)." Listed children include **Harmon Miles Riggs 1834–1874**, Catharine Riggs 1836–1913, Hibbard M. Riggs 1840–…
- Ancestry.co.uk snippet (matches FamilySearch tree): "Father: David J RIGGS. Mother: Catherine M HENDRICKS."
- **Verdict:**
  - David J. Riggs Jr (1804–1850) + **Catherine M. Hendricks** → **VERIFIED** as a couple and as parents of Harmon Miles Riggs (FamilySearch tree + Ancestry tree agree; redriverancestry independently places Harmon Miles Riggs 1834–1874 with wife Amelia, closing the loop).
  - **Parents of David J. Riggs Jr** (David Phillip Riggs + Elizabeth Jayne) → **INFERENCE** (single FamilySearch tree; no primary US record checked in this pass).

### 1d. Provenance caveats for the Riggs line
- FamilySearch person-page data is **genealogist-curated tree data**, not a primary record. The generational chain Ernest→Harmon→David is corroborated across FamilySearch + redriverancestry + WikiTree + WeRelate, but the underlying US primary records (NY/Ontario birth, Wisconsin 1858 marriage, US censuses) were **not individually pulled** this pass.
- The Riggs family clearly migrated **US → Red River** (Harmon m. 1858 in Wisconsin; son Ernest m. 1887 in Portage la Prairie). This is a US-origin line, distinct from the Quebec/Red-River Métis families — verify the US side if stricter proof is wanted.
- Recommended primary checks: Wisconsin marriage index (Jackson Co., 1858), US censuses 1840–1860 for David Jr / Harmon, Ernest Charles Riggs's Manitoba vital stats (birth/death) and his scrip/Census 1891/1901 household.

## 2. CATHERINE PARENTEAU (wife of Henry Hallett Jr; mother of Catherine Hallett)

### 2a. Who she is — **VERIFIED**
- **Catherine Parenteau** (c1799–1857) married **Henry Hallett Jr** (1799–1871) on **18 Oct 1824, St John's, Red River Settlement.** Henry Jr was son of Henry ("Sir") Hallett Sr (1773–1844), the HBC/NWC trader, + Catherine Crise (Cree).
- Together they were parents of **Catherine Hallett (1824–1880)**, who married David Spence on 15 Feb 1844. This is the direct line to Bayard.
- Sources (agree on the marriage + Catherine Hallett as daughter):
  - archive.org Exovedes genealogy ("Ancestors of li Exovedes"): "He married Catherine Parenteau on 18 October 1824 at St-John's, RRS" (Henry Hallett Jr entry, 12 children incl. Catherine Hallett 1824–1880).
  - redriverancestry `HALLETT-HENRY-1773.php`: "Son Henry also married Catherine PARENTEAU that day [18 Oct 1824] …" and SPENCE page: Catherine Hallett "daughter of Catherine PARENTEAU and Henry HALLETT (1799-1871)."
  - academia.edu "Manitoba's Provisional Government of 1870 / Convention of Forty": Catherine Hallett "daughter of Henry Hallett and Catherine Parenteau."
  - Find A Grave `235841118` (Esther Justine Hallett Klyne): parents **Henry Hallett II (1799–1871)** and **Catherine Parenteau Hallett (1799–1857)**.
  - WikiTree `Klyne-21`: Catherine Parenteau abt 1799 – abt 3 Mar 1857, Quebec, Canada.
- **Birth year note:** Free sources give **c1799** (WikiTree, FindAGrave, Klyne). The task brief said "1806"; treat **c1799** as the better-supported figure. Death **1857** is consistent across sources.

### 2b. Her PARENTAGE — **NOT CONCLUSIVELY RESOLVED** (the open question)
Two competing, both-incomplete leads in free sources:

- **Lead A — "Jean Baptiste Parenteau of Quebec" (best explicit claim):**
  - redriverancestry `HALLETT-HENRY-1773.php` is the ONLY free source that names a father: "daughter of Jean Baptiste PARENTEAU from Quebec." No mother named. This is a single hobbyist claim → **INFERENCE**, unverified against a primary record.
- **Lead B — "Joseph Parenteau Sr + Suzanne Richard (Cree)" (task's hypothesis):**
  - This is a well-documented Red River Métis Parenteau couple: children incl. Joseph Parenteau (abt 1810–1890, WikiTree Parenteau-303), Pierre Parenteau (1811–1893, Metis Museum Barkwell "son of Joseph Parenteau Sr. and his Cree wife Susanne"), Joseph Dodet/Daudais Parenteau, Adelaide "Marguerite" (1812–), Jean-Baptiste, Gabriel, Louis, Judith, etc.
  - **However, NO free source explicitly names Catherine Parenteau (wife of Henry Hallett Jr) as a daughter of this couple.** It is plausible (right family, right generation, Red River geography) but **UNCONFIRMED → INFERENCE/hypothesis only.**
  - Note: researchers themselves disagree on this family (a Métis Family Resources FB thread flags that the "Joseph Victor Parenteau + Suzanne Cree" link "is incorrect" and points to "Joseph Parenteau + Suzanne Daigneault" — so even the wife's surname is contested).
- **WikiTree `Parenteau-571`** (Catherine Parenteau Hallett): father unknown, mother unknown — i.e., the tree editors explicitly left her parents unresolved.

### 2c. Verdict on Parenteau parentage
- **Resolved:** Catherine Parenteau (c1799–1857) = wife of Henry Hallett Jr, mother of Catherine Hallett. **VERIFIED.**
- **Unresolved:** her parents. Best explicit free-source lead = **Jean Baptiste Parenteau of Quebec** (redriverancestry, INFERENCE). The **Joseph Parenteau Sr + Suzanne Richard** hypothesis is plausible but unsupported by any source that ties Catherine to them. **A primary record is required to settle it** — the highest-value targets are:
  1. **Catherine Parenteau's scrip affidavit / application** (1875–76, with her father's name) — LAC Collection Search / metisnationdatabase.ca.
  2. Her baptism record (parish register, ~1799, likely Quebec or a NWC post) or the 18 Oct 1824 marriage record's parents column.
  3. Métis Museum / Barkwell "Company Men" Hallett (Vol. 14) and Parenteau volumes (pay/print) — the curated Parenteau line.
- **Recommendation for the tree:** keep Catherine Parenteau's parents **blank/pending** in `family-tree.json` (do NOT attach Joseph Parenteau + Suzanne Richard without a source that links Catherine to them). Add the redriverancestry "Jean Baptiste Parenteau" lead as a research note only.
